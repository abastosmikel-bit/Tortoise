# Host protocol v2 study: decisions

Each decision is recorded **before** the runs it governs, unless marked otherwise. Circuit throughout (frozen): Variant 3 at
`fitc10` (fitc10_campaign D9/D11), weights `rev10/eval/F_hw_m4_firstpass.npy`, `RELAX=1` solver set. **Only the host
protocol changes.** Folder: `results/host_v2/`; earlier result folders are imported read-only, never modified.

## H0. Tooling (2026-10-28)
- `scripts/hv2.py` imports `energy_host/scripts/eh.py` (and through it `fitc10_campaign/scripts/common.py`) read-only.
  Every netlist is `eh.build(...)` (Variant 3 resize, corners, MC) plus host-side changes only: power-up ramps
  (`eh` option `cold`), `rstb` release time, reset extension, T₁, race window, an ideal host model (B-sources, not part
  of the die) for the early race end, and an optional start state (`.ic` of the saved nodes) for a power-up that follows
  a finite VDD-off period.
- **Simulation-only change:** the transient stops 5 µs after the host's read time (ngspice `stop when` on the host
  timer). The first test (#81, nominal) reproduces the energy/host study's O12 run of #81 (race 1788.4 vs 1788.1 µs; the 0.3 µs is
  the `rstb` change; same trips, lock latency 33.05 vs 33.09 µs, VDD energy 661 vs 662 nJ).
- Metrics: `common.metrics` with trip counting from 320 µs + reset extension and the rail reset level read at
  311 µs + extension; plus the **host-observed outcome at the read time** (latched = `na` > 2.5 V at the read time;
  valid = exactly one latched and it is the first output to rise). Failure labels as `summarize.py::failure`.
- VDD-off period: from the read-time state, VDD, rows, `rstr`, `rstb`, `phib` ramp to 0 V over 10 µs (`phi` is 0 V),
  then every source stays at 0 V; free time step (max step T_off/400).
- Per-run JSONs in git; `*.csv.gz` (1 µs waveforms), `*.cir.gz`, `*_draws.json` in Git LFS. *(Public release: these files are not included; they can be regenerated by re-running the campaign, see [REPRODUCE.md](../../REPRODUCE.md) §6.)*

## H1. Pre-freeze interaction checks (declared 2026-10-28 before their results were read; development runs, not results)
Training samples only (the first training sample of each class: train idx 10, 2, 0, 7, 6), global corners, no
mismatch, `raw/dev/`. They decide between protocol options; their outcome is recorded in H1-outcome before H2 is frozen.
1. **VDD-off decay** (#81 end state at nominal, 10 s off): what state does a power-up after T_off start from?
2. **a1 under power gating** (nominal, µ ×0.5, |V_th| 1.0 V, 0.85/×0.7): all-zero start vs the 1 s-off state vs the
   energy/host study's warm a1; max time step 0.1 vs 1 µs (simulation cost: 840 s vs 20 s per a1 run).
3. **µ estimator** from a1's three-point fit (a² ∝ µ): one-pass vs two-pass extraction, first explored on the energy/host study's
   stored a1 curves of the 25 B4 cells (development use of existing data, disclosed here).
4. **A (nominal, k = 1):** all-zero start vs 1 s-off start vs `rstb` at 150 µs (effect of (e) under cold start).
5. **B (µ ×0.5 at 0.65 V):** k = 2 with D/READ scaled by k vs fixed 50/10 µs; k = 2 without the reset extension;
   k = 1 (no µ compensation).
6. **Start-state chain:** #81 chained classification → 1 s off → classification, to test convergence of the start state.

## H1-outcome (recorded 2026-10-28 ≈ 21:40 UTC, after the H1 runs, before any evaluation run)
Sources: `raw/dev/`, `raw/cal/dev_*`, `raw/cal/design_nominal/`, `summary/dev_chain.json`, `logs/dev_*.log`.
1. **VDD-off decay** (`raw/dev/dev_off_s81_10s.json`): all five rails fall together (no class memory) to 0.62 V after
   1 ms, 0.47 V (10 ms), 0.34 V (100 ms), **0.23 V (1 s)**, 0.11 V (10 s); `ref` 0.15 V at 1 s. The latch nodes `na`/`nb`
   and `lockb` are capacitively pulled **below 0 V** (to −2.0 V) by the 10 µs power-down ramp and recover over ≈ 10 s;
   this is a new stress condition, audited in C2. Consequence: a "cold" start is not an all-zero start unless the die
   is off for ≫ 10 s. **Fixed for v2: T_off = 1 s between classifications** (the 1 /s rate of the energy/host study's energy
   headline), and every classification starts from the simulated 1 s-off state.
2. **a1 under power gating.** The cold power-up changes the a1 measurement: rails start at 0.07 V instead of the warm
   reset floor (0.9 V), and the energy/host study's offset no longer applies (raw nominal 0.599 V cold vs 0.618 V warm). The
   the energy/host study three-point |V_th| fit still works when its offset is re-derived under the v2 sequence (errors −1.2…+6.6 mV
   on the 6 development corners). Max step 1 µs vs 0.1 µs: same estimate to 0.01 mV (20 s vs 183 s per run), so 1 µs
   is used. Start state (all-zero vs 1 s-off): +4.1 mV, µ +1.5 %. A rail precharge pulse before the ramp (tried to
   mimic the warm floor) made it much worse (−159 mV at µ ×0.5) and is not used.
3. **µ estimator.** On the energy/host study's stored warm-start a1 curves a two-pass extraction looked good, but under the v2
   cold sequence it is worse than one pass (−36 mV at 1.0 V/µ ×0.5) and a² from the fit is unusable as a µ estimate
   (µ̂ 1.12 at 1.0 V/µ ×1, 0.73 at 1.0 V/µ ×0.5). **Chosen: µ̂ = ΔI_DD at V_SG = V_raw + 0.6 V, divided by the same
   quantity on the nominal die.** Development-corner errors +0 to +12 % (biased high at low µ: R_C does not scale with µ;
   no correction is applied). These corners overlap the C3 grid (0.65/1.0 V × µ 1/0.5, 0.85/0.7, 0.75/0.85); this is
   disclosed: the estimator was chosen from 4 candidates on them, with no fitted constant beyond the nominal-die values.
4. **A (nominal, k = 1, 5 training samples):** all-zero start, 1 s-off start and `rstb` 150 µs give **identical outcomes
   on 5/5** (4 valid, 1 near-tie double latch on train #7 in all three). The 1 s-off start shortens the race by
   ≈ 260 µs (rails start at 0.23 V); `rstb` 50 vs 150 µs changes the race by 0.3 µs under cold start.
5. **B (µ ×0.5, 0.65 V):** k = 1 times out (no race finishes in 2500 µs; 2/2 so far); k = 2 with a **fixed** 50/10 µs
   early end gives `none_latched` (the read comes before the slowed latch completes; 2/2); k = 2 with **D and READ scaled
   by k** is valid (2/2); without the reset extension also valid, race 11 µs shorter. **Fixed: D = 50·k µs and
   READ = 10·k µs.** The race at µ ×0.5 with k = 2 takes 3.7–3.9 ms from a 1 s-off start, against cold-start races up to
   2.17 ms at µ ×1 (energy/host study O2, 90 samples): a 2500·k window leaves too little margin when µ̂ reads high (k < 1/µ).
   **Fixed: the race window (timeout bound) is 4000·k µs.** It has no energy cost (the early end stops the race).
   The remaining B runs (3 samples) finish after the freeze; they are reported but cannot change H2.
6. **Start-state chain** (#81 ×3, 1 s off): steps 0–2 give the same race (1525.634 µs), rail reset level (0.334 V) and
   `ref` (1.170 V) to 0.001 µs / 0.01 mV. The 1 s-off state carries no measurable history into the next classification
   (rails equalise at 0.231 V; the `na` differences it carries are erased by the `rstb` precharge).
7. **End-to-end calibration** on off-grid corners (0.80 V/µ ×0.55, 0.95 V/µ ×0.90): |V_th| error +0.8 / −2.3 mV, µ̂ error
   +6.3 / +0.7 %, race cross-check +15.6 / +29.2 mV (not flagged).

## H2. HOST PROTOCOL v2 (FROZEN 2026-10-28; changes only as dated deviations in H4)
Implementation: `scripts/hv2.py` (constants at its top), `scripts/phases.py`. Times in µs. The host uses only: its own
line levels and timer, the five class outputs `na1–5` (logic threshold 1.65 V, "latched" > 2.5 V), and a current meter
on its own VDD supply (I_DD). The simulator's |V_th| and µ are used only in runs labelled **ideal**.

### H2.1 Calibration session (once per die/condition, before its classifications; re-run whenever a re-trim is wanted)
1. **Start:** die unpowered for a long time (simulated as every node at 0 V).
2. **a1, supply-current transfer:** 0–10 µs VDD, rows and `phi` ramp 0 → 3.3 V; `rstr` 0 V from t = 0 (rails clamped by
   the reset switches, latches held), back to 3.3 V at 4302–4303; `rstb` 0 V until 50, 3.3 V from 51; `phib` 0 V until
   290, 3.3 V from 291 (`phi` the complement); rows 3.3 V until 300, linear ramp to 1.3 V at 4300, back to 3.3 V at
   4301. ΔI = I_DD − mean I_DD over 293–300. V_SG = 3.3 V − row level.
   - V_raw: V_SG at ΔI = 5, 20, 45 µA (first crossing, linear interpolation), exact solve of √I = a(V_SG − V − I·R).
   - **V̂ = V_raw − c_a1**, c_a1 = **−50.883 mV** (nominal die, design-time, `summary/design_constants.json`).
   - **µ̂ = ΔI(V_SG = V_raw + 0.6 V) / I_ref**, I_ref = **31.439 µA** (nominal die). If any crossing is missing the
     calibration fails (reported; the die is not classified with learned values).
3. VDD and all lines off (10 µs ramp to 0 V), **1 s**.
4. **Race-timing cross-check:** two classifications (H2.2) with input x = 0 and the learned V₁, k, and
   V₂ = 3.3 − V̂ − 0.45 (race a) and 3.3 − V̂ − 0.30 (race b), each followed by 1 s off. The host times the first
   output edge from the start of C2 (t_a, t_b). V̂_race = (r·B − A)/(r − 1) − c_race, r = √(t_b/t_a), A = 3.3 − V₂a,
   B = 3.3 − V₂b, c_race = **−34.481 mV** (nominal die). **Flag** if a time is missing or |V̂_race − V̂| > 50 mV. A flag
   is reported; it does not change the levels (a1 is the primary estimate).
5. **Learned values:** V₁ = 3.3 − V̂ − 0.83, V₂ = 3.3 − V̂ − 0.25 (rounded to 1 mV); **k = clip(1/µ̂, 1.0, 2.5)**.
6. The state after the 1 s off that follows race b is the start state of the first classification.

### H2.2 Classification (every classification; e = 300·(k − 1))
1. **Power-up** from the 1 s-off state: 0–10 µs VDD, rows and `phi` ramp 0 → protocol level.
2. **Reset:** `rstr` 0 V from t = 0 to 311.5 + e, 3.3 V by 312.5 + e. **`rstb` 0 V until 50, 3.3 V by 51 (not scaled).**
   `phib` 0 V until 309 + e, 3.3 V by 310 + e (`phi` the complement). Rows 3.3 V until 301 + e. (reset length 300·k)
3. **Sampling/calibration** (not scaled): rows at V₁ from 302 + e to 310 + e (1 µs edges).
4. **C1** from t₀ = 312.5 + e: row i at V₁ for 20 + x_i·170·k (WMIN 20 µs not scaled), all rows off 5 µs.
5. **C2** from t_C2 = t₀ + 20 + 170·k + 5: all rows at V₂.
6. **Early end:** the host watches `na1–5` from 320 + e. At the first output > 1.65 V (time t_f) it waits **50·k**, returns
   every row to 3.3 V (0.2 µs edge) and **reads the outputs 10·k later**. Result = the classes with `na` > 2.5 V at the
   read. **Valid** = exactly one class latched and it is the first output that rose; otherwise the failure label
   (`timeout`, `none_latched`, `multi_latched`, `lockout_failed`, `latched_not_first`, `stuck_latched`).
7. **Timeout:** no output by t_C2 + **4000·k** → no result (`timeout`).
8. **Power gating:** after the read, VDD and all lines → 0 V (10 µs ramp); off **1 s** before the next classification.
9. Energy per classification = Σ over the 11 sources of ∫ v·i dt from t = 0 to the read time (energy/host study E1 definition);
   standby = 0 (no source is on); energy returned during the power-down is not credited.

### H2.3 Reference policies (labelled)
- **ideal:** H2.2 with V̂ = true |V_th| and k = clip(1/µ_true, 1, 2.5), same start state as the learned runs.
- **v1** (energy/host study "baseline"): VDD on, warm start (Variant 3 warm state), `rstb` 150 µs, 300 µs reset, T₁ 170,
  2500 µs window, levels fixed (1.82/2.40 V) or ideal re-trim. Existing v1 runs are reused, not re-simulated.

## H3. Phase rules (declared with H2, before any evaluation run)
- **Statistics:** accuracy = correct of N (invalid = wrong), Wilson 95 %; validity likewise; "strict" also counts a
  first output before C2 as a failure. Paired v1/v2 comparisons: exact two-sided McNemar on correct/incorrect.
- **C1:** the nominal die runs its own calibration session; it is also the design-time die, so its estimates equal the
  design constants **by construction** (stated in the report). 90 test samples, each from the calibration start state,
  instrumented. Chain: test samples 30–39 back to back (1 s off between). v1 = fitc10 Variant 3 Phase 1 (`raw/v3/phase1`)
  and chain. Energy: from the C1 runs; calibration-session energy reported separately and amortised over N.
- **C2:** fitc10 Phase 2 input picks (maxsum, minsum, maxcol, mincol) + #31 after #30 (1 s off); each run audited over
  the power-up and classification, and over the power-down + 1 s off; every TFT, max |V_GS| and |V_GD| vs 3.5 V; the
  most negative node voltage is reported.
- **C3:** 25 cells (ΔV_th 0, 0.10, 0.20, 0.30, 0.35 V from 0.65 V × µ ×1, 0.85, 0.7, 0.6, 0.5; global corners, no
  mismatch; aging PROXIES), each with its own calibration session; the 20-sample subset. **A cell fails** if its correct
  count is ≤ (the v2 nominal cell's count) − 3, or a calibration fails or is flagged. Only failing cells get the ideal
  reference (20 runs each). v1 comparison: the energy/host study's B4 (3 × 3, none/ideal) and its E11.2 run.
- **C4:** 3B-V dies (anchor 20 mV, `pelgrom_vth`, seeds 20001–20005, nominal global corner), each die calibrates itself;
  20 samples. v1 = fitc10 3B-V anchor-20 runs, paired per die and sample.
- **C5:** cell 0.65 V/µ ×0.5 with its C3 calibration; the 20 subset samples in subset order as one chain (1 s off
  between): (i) v2; (ii) v2 with the v1 reset length (e = 0, everything else unchanged). Compared with the C3 independent
  runs of that cell: outcome changes, race shift, rail level at the end of reset.
- **C6:** |V_th| 0.45, 0.47, 0.49, 0.51, 0.53, 0.55 V, µ ×1, each with its own calibration; 20 samples. v1 = the energy/host study's B5.
- **Order:** C1 → C2 → C3 → C4 → C5 (sequential chain, alongside) → C6.

## H4. Deviations after the freeze (dated; each states what it affects)
- **H4.1 (2026-10-29 ≈ 00:50 UTC) — simulation setting, not protocol.** On mismatch die an20 d01 (C4 calibration), the
  1 s VDD-off simulation after race a stalled in its DC operating point (19 min without progress, normally 3 s; the
  same netlist runs with `uic` or `NOOPITER`). The no-data record it left was deleted and `hv2.simulate` now retries
  any run that produces no data once with `.options NOOPITER` (gmin stepping without the direct Newton step); each such
  run records `run.fallback` and its first attempt. Check on the nominal case: the forced-NOOPITER off state equals the
  normal one to 6·10⁻¹¹ V (`raw/dev/chk_off_*`). **Affects:** only runs that record `run.fallback` (listed in the report);
  runs that converged normally are unchanged. The protocol (H2) is unchanged.
  Amendment (2026-10-29 ≈ 01:20 UTC): the first attempt of a VDD-off simulation now times out after 60 s (normal runs
  take ≈ 3 s) instead of 600 s, because every off-period on die d01 stalled; compute only, no result changes.
- **H4.2 (2026-10-29 ≈ 02:10 UTC) — the early-end timer, as simulated.** H2.2 step 6 says the host waits 50·k µs "at the
  first output > 1.65 V". The host model in every v2 run (inherited from the energy/host study's O1/O12) is an integrator: it counts
  the **accumulated time during which any output is above 1.65 V** (from 320 + e µs) and returns the rows to 3.3 V when
  that count reaches 50·k µs; the read follows 0.2 + 10·k µs later. The two agree whenever every `na` is below 1.65 V
  when counting starts and the first output stays high (all C1/C2/C5 runs and all but one C3 run so far). They differ
  when an `na` is still decaying through 1.65 V at 320 + e µs (found in C6 diagnostics; mismatch die d04; low |V_th|):
  the integrator counts that time, so the rows go off earlier after the real trip; a literal "first output" host would
  instead end the race at 320 + e + 50·k µs. **Decision:** the integrator is the v2 rule (it is what was simulated and
  frozen in code). The metric's read time is corrected to the simulated one (t_read = rows-off + 0.2 + READ =
  stop time − 5 µs) in `metrics_v2`, and `scripts/read_fix.py` recomputes the read-time outcome of earlier runs from
  their 1 µs waveforms where the stored read time is later than (stop time − 5 µs) by more than 0.15 µs (one time step of `stop when` overshoot) (the old values are kept in the JSON as `read_fix`).
  **Applied 02:30 UTC: 0 of 440 finished classification runs changed** (C1, C2 chain step, C3 so far, C5; the largest
  pre-trip count among them is 0.05 µs, C3 0.65 V/µ ×1 #60, below the 0.15 µs tolerance). A first pass of `read_fix.py`
  with a 0.01 µs tolerance rewrote 393 JSONs by ≤ 0.1 µs (the `stop when` overshoot) with no outcome change; it was
  reverted the same minute. **Affects:** the d04 calibration races (6 µs; race times unaffected), the C6 calibration
  races (stuck, failing under either reading), and any later C4/C6 run with a pre-trip high `na`; the report gives, for
  C6 and C4, how many runs a literal-"first output" host would have aborted.
  Final check (05:20 UTC): `read_fix.py` over all 500 C3, 100 C4 and 180 C6/C6b runs changed 0 (C4 and C6 ran with the
  corrected metric; their pre-trip counts are in each JSON as `host_timer_pre_trip_us`: d04 6.1 µs on 20/20 runs,
  C6b 26.6 / 13.2 / 2.1 µs at 0.57 / 0.59 / 0.61 V).
