# Tortoise: a p-type-only OTFT time-race classifier (v1.0, simulation study)

## 1. What Tortoise is

Tortoise is a 6-input × 5-class linear classifier designed to be built only from p-type organic thin-film transistors
(DNTT-family OTFTs on an AlOx/SAM gate dielectric) on a single 3.3 V rail, with a 3.5 V dielectric stress limit and
134 transistors ([phase2_audit.json](results/fitc10_campaign/summary/v3/phase2_audit.json), `n_devices`). Weights are integer numbers of 10 µm transistor fingers per input–class pair. Each class integrates
its weighted input current onto a rail capacitor; then all rails ramp together and the first rail to reach the
comparator threshold latches and locks out the others ("integrate, then race"). A host drives the supply, ten timing
and level lines, and reads the five class outputs.

Tortoise v1.0 is the current, consistent definition of the design: the base circuit resized for a published
C10-DNTT device corner (`fitc10`), the hardware-aware weights of the base design, and host protocol v2.1, which learns
the die's threshold voltage and mobility from its own supply current and gates the supply between classifications.
The full specification is [`docs/SPEC.md`](docs/SPEC.md).

## 2. Status

- **Simulation only.** Nothing has been fabricated or measured. All results are ngspice-42 transients of a compact
  OTFT model.
- **No hardware.** The device corner is a reconstruction from a published dataset measured on Si, not on foil.
- **Synthetic dataset.** 300 generated samples (210 training, 90 test) of a 6-feature "wound panel"
  (`clinical_validation: false` in [`data/trace2_data.npz`](data/trace2_data.npz), generated by
  [`data/generate_wound_data.py`](data/generate_wound_data.py)); no patient or sensor data.
- The research phase is finished; this repository is the consolidated record.

## 3. Headline results

Each line links to its row in [`RESULTS_INDEX.md`](RESULTS_INDEX.md) (ID in brackets) and to the summary file it is
read from. `tools/check_results_index.py` checks every number in the index against its summary file.

1. **Nominal accuracy:** 62/90 = 68.9 % (Wilson 95 % 58.7–77.5 %), validity 87/90, N = 90 test samples
   ([FC-01, FC-02](RESULTS_INDEX.md); [phase1.json](results/fitc10_campaign/summary/v3/phase1.json)). Host protocol v2
   gives the same 90 outcomes ([H2-01](RESULTS_INDEX.md); [c1_nominal.json](results/host_v2/summary/c1_nominal.json)).
2. **Threshold and mobility drift (aging proxies) with the learned host protocol:** 351/500 = 70.2 % pooled over 25
   cells (|V_th| 0.65–1.00 V × µ ×1–×0.5), every cell 13–15 of 20, N = 20 per cell
   ([H2-06](RESULTS_INDEX.md); [c3_aging.json](results/host_v2/summary/c3_aging.json)). Without re-trimming, a
   +0.20 V shift gives 0/20 ([EH-11](RESULTS_INDEX.md); [b4_aging.json](results/energy_host/summary/b4_aging.json)).
3. **Low-threshold edge (v2.1):** 15/20 at each |V_th| from 0.51 to 0.61 V, 0/20 at 0.49 V, N = 20 per point
   ([V21-01, V21-02](RESULTS_INDEX.md); [e1_lower_edge.json](results/host_v21/summary/e1_lower_edge.json)).
4. **Assumed local mismatch:** 64/100 and 57/100 at size-scaled σ_Vth anchors of 20 and 35 mV; 31–41 of 100 under the
   flat-σ worst case, N = 100 per level (5 dies × 20)
   ([FC-13, FC-14, FC-15](RESULTS_INDEX.md); [phase3B_V.json](results/fitc10_campaign/summary/v3/phase3B_V.json),
   [phase3B.json](results/fitc10_campaign/summary/v3/phase3B.json)).
5. **Energy:** 588 nJ per classification (median, N = 90) with zero standby under power gating, against 714 nJ plus
   98–199 µW standby with the supply left on ([H2-02, EH-01, EH-03](RESULTS_INDEX.md);
   [c1_nominal.json](results/host_v2/summary/c1_nominal.json), [b1_energy.json](results/energy_host/summary/b1_energy.json)).
6. **Dielectric stress:** 0 of 134 TFTs above 3.5 V in every audited run, including supply ramps from 1 to 100 µs;
   worst 3.436 V at the 1 µs ramp, 5 runs per condition ([FC-07, H2-05, V21-07](RESULTS_INDEX.md);
   [e2_ramp.json](results/host_v21/summary/e2_ramp.json)).
7. **Weights are not the limit:** retrained weights gave 62/90 again (6 gained, 6 lost, McNemar p = 1.0, N = 90); the
   bias-free crossbar form reaches 67.9 % in training CV against 73.7 % for a general linear classifier
   ([WS-05, WS-07, WS-01, WS-02](RESULTS_INDEX.md); [A2_FINAL_frozen.json](results/weights_schemeA/summary/A2_FINAL_frozen.json),
   [A0_ceilings.json](results/weights_schemeA/summary/A0_ceilings.json)).

A plain-language account of the concept, its evidence and its limits, with every number tagged to its source, is in
[`CONCEPT.md`](CONCEPT.md) (checked by `tools/check_concept.py`).

## 4. Operating envelope (summary)

Under host protocol v2.1, with no local mismatch, the design classified at 13–15 of 20 on the fixed 20-sample subset
over |V_th| 0.51–1.00 V at µ ×1, and over |V_th| 0.65–1.00 V at every tested µ from ×1 to ×0.5
([e1_lower_edge.json](results/host_v21/summary/e1_lower_edge.json), [c3_aging.json](results/host_v2/summary/c3_aging.json)).
It fails at 0.49 V; above 1.00 V, low |V_th| combined with µ loss, and mismatch with drift were not simulated under
v2/v2.1. The full table, with N and a source for every cell, and the host rules that produce it, are in
[`docs/SPEC.md`](docs/SPEC.md) §4–5.

## 5. Limitations

From the "what this does not show" sections of the campaign reports:
- **No measurement of anything.** Device corner, mismatch and aging are not calibrated to measured data of this process.
- **Reconstructed corner and sizing.** `fitc10` comes from C10-DNTT on Si with currents read by eye (±0.3 µA);
  JOFF and LOV were not fitted. The original corner card and netlist were lost; the Variant 3 sizing was rebuilt from
  the author's decisions, one of them made after seeing data ([fitc10 REPORT §4](results/fitc10_campaign/REPORT.md)).
- **Synthetic data, small N.** N = 90 gives a Wilson interval of about ±10 points (58.7–77.5 % around 68.9 %;
  [phase1.json](results/fitc10_campaign/summary/v3/phase1.json)); most robustness points use N = 20, where one sample
  is 5 points.
- **Assumed mismatch, proxy aging.** No measured matching or bias-stress data exist for this stack. Mismatch results
  depend strongly on the assumed model: 31–41 % (flat σ) vs 57–64 % (size-scaled V_th) on the same circuit
  ([phase3B.json](results/fitc10_campaign/summary/v3/phase3B.json), [phase3B_V.json](results/fitc10_campaign/summary/v3/phase3B_V.json)). Aging is a uniform global shift.
- **Ideal host.** Noise-free supply-current meter and timer, ideal drivers, 1 mV level resolution; design-time
  constants come from the same model they correct ([host_v2 REPORT §10](results/host_v2/REPORT.md)).
- **Known failure modes** ([`docs/SPEC.md`](docs/SPEC.md) §6): the 0.49 V edge and 174–209 µW standby for dies
  that must keep the supply on ([e1_lower_edge.json](results/host_v21/summary/e1_lower_edge.json)); near-tie double
  latches, the main loss everywhere ([c3_aging.json](results/host_v2/summary/c3_aging.json)); internal nodes at
  ≈ −2.2 V after power-down ([c2_stress.json](results/host_v2/summary/c2_stress.json)); a 64 mV stress margin at
  1 µs supply ramps ([e2_ramp.json](results/host_v21/summary/e2_ramp.json)); false alarms of the race cross-check at
  high |V_th| ([c3_aging.json](results/host_v2/summary/c3_aging.json)).
- **Not modelled:** layout parasitics, IR drop, noise, pads/ESD, host electronics. Absolute race times are
  model-dependent; ordering and gaps are the robust outputs.
- **Lab-corner numbers** of the base design ([`docs/TOOLING.md`](docs/TOOLING.md) §3) have no summary files in this
  repository and are not used as results here.
- **Waveforms not included.** Simulated waveforms, netlists and Monte Carlo draw files are not part of this
  repository; they are regenerated by re-running the campaigns ([`REPRODUCE.md`](REPRODUCE.md) §6).

## 6. Design lessons

The transferable findings, each with evidence and scope, are in [`docs/DESIGN_LESSONS.md`](docs/DESIGN_LESSONS.md).
In short:
- **Placeholder models hide gate capacitance.** The Level-1 card behind an earlier design iteration had essentially
  none; the real value is 30–300× larger and that design did not work with a physical model
  ([`docs/TOOLING.md`](docs/TOOLING.md) §3.1; lesson 1).
- **Window ≈ V_DD − 2|V_th|** without negative bias: `ref` − rail reset falls from 0.477 V to 0.323 V as |V_th| goes from
  0.65 to 1.05 V ([phase3A_T.json](results/fitc10_campaign/summary/v3/phase3A_T.json); lesson 2).
- **Re-trim is mandatory:** with host levels fixed, every |V_th| ≥ 0.85 V gives 0/20; re-trimmed, 0.85 V gives 14/20
  ([phase3A.json](results/fitc10_campaign/summary/v3/phase3A.json), [phase3A_T.json](results/fitc10_campaign/summary/v3/phase3A_T.json);
  lesson 3).
- **Accuracy is limited by the architecture, not the weights:** 67.9 % ceiling for the bias-free crossbar vs 73.7 %
  for a general linear classifier ([A0_ceilings.json](results/weights_schemeA/summary/A0_ceilings.json); lesson 4).
- **Near ties are set by latch regeneration, not lock latency** (resolve threshold 8.70 µs vs 19.2 µs lock latency;
  [A0_calibration.json](results/weights_schemeA/summary/A0_calibration.json), [phase1.json](results/fitc10_campaign/summary/v3/phase1.json);
  lesson 7).
- **Static current sets the energy; gating time is the lever** ([b1_energy.json](results/energy_host/summary/b1_energy.json);
  lesson 11).

## 8. How to reproduce

See [`REPRODUCE.md`](REPRODUCE.md). In short: `apt-get install ngspice`, clone, `sh tools/setup_layout.sh`, then
`sh tools/reproduce_summaries.sh` (rebuilds every summary from the committed per-run JSONs and checks the index, about
12 s) and `sh tools/smoke_test.sh` (five real SPICE runs that must reproduce recorded outcomes, about 3 min on 4
cores).

## 9. Design history

- **An earlier design iteration** was designed and checked with a Level-1 MOSFET placeholder model; it does not work
  with a physical OTFT model. It is not part of this release; its lessons are in
  [`docs/DESIGN_LESSONS.md`](docs/DESIGN_LESSONS.md) (lesson 1) and [`docs/TOOLING.md`](docs/TOOLING.md) §3.1.
- **The base design** redesigned the circuit for a charge-based OTFT model at a lab corner (no negative bias, host
  timing, "integrate, then race"); see [`docs/TOOLING.md`](docs/TOOLING.md).
- **Tortoise v1.0** fixes the `fitc10` corner, the Variant 3 sizing, the base-design weights and host protocol v2.1, as
  tested by the campaigns in [`results/`](results/); defined in [`docs/SPEC.md`](docs/SPEC.md).
